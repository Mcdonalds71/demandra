CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

Can we pay in Nigerian Naira (NGN)?

Yes. Nigerian corporate entities can be billed in Naira through our local entity (Demandra Technologies Ltd, RC 1928410). We peg invoices quarterly based on the official Central Bank of Nigeria FX rate.

How fast can our store network go live?

Most retail chains complete initial POS ingestion within 48 hours using our pre-built Odoo, Dynamics, or CSV adapters. Baseline demand forecasts become active across all stores within 72 hours of historical receipt verification.

Is our SKU sales velocity visible to other retail competitors?

Strictly no. Every enterprise workspace operates inside an isolated Azure Blob container encrypted with dedicated tenant keys. We never blend, aggregate, or train public models using your commercial sales data.

What if we add new store locations midway through our annual contract?

You can provision new store locations in seconds from your console. Additional locations are pro-rated to the remainder of your annual billing cycle without service interruption.
