Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Demandra Labs Ltd ("Demandra", "we", "us") provides an AI-assisted platform that helps retailers and wholesalers forecast demand. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, work email, organization name, and role.
  • Sales data you connect — sales history, inventory levels, and SKU catalog data.
  • Usage data — how you interact with the product, used to improve reliability and features.
  • Technical data — device, browser, and log information collected to keep the service secure.

How we use your information

  • To forecast demand and flag stockout or overstock risk.
  • To generate reorder recommendations from your data.
  • To operate, secure, maintain, and improve the platform.
  • To communicate about your account, updates, and support.

Data protection & security

Your sales data is yours. Each organization's data is isolated in its own workspace, encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not sell your data, and we do not use one organization's data to train models shared with another.

You are responsible for having the appropriate basis to connect your sales and inventory data to Demandra. We apply role-based access controls, audit logging, and least-privilege practices across our infrastructure.

AI processing

Demand forecasting and reorder recommendations are performed by AI models running on our secured cloud infrastructure. Forecasts are decision-support, not a guarantee — your team should always review recommendations before ordering.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers who help us operate the platform (such as cloud hosting and analytics), under contracts that require them to protect it, and where required by law.

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email privacy@demandra.io or write to Demandra Labs Ltd, Data Protection Office.
