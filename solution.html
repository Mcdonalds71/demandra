01 // BASKET VELOCITY MODELING

Predict store depletion 14 days before shelves go empty.

Traditional retail inventory relies on retrospective averages — what sold last month or last quarter. In volatile markets like Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt, consumer purchase behavior changes weekly based on local liquidity, regional payday schedules, inflation shifts, and transit gridlocks.

Demandra ingest POS transaction logs at the receipt line level every 15 minutes. Our models decompose item co-purchases, basket depth, and time-of-day traffic patterns to project SKU depletion curves across every individual shelf.

  • Forecast Granularity Store-SKU-Day
  • Forecast Horizon 14 to 60 Days Rolling
  • Model Accuracy Rate 98.2% on top 20% FMCG volume

02 // INTER-STORE BALANCING

Re-route stock between stores before incurring fresh reorder costs.

In multi-store retail chains, one store often faces a critical stockout on premium beverages or imported staples while another branch 12 kilometers away holds 45 days of dormant stock.

Demandra identifies store-to-store variance in real time. Instead of triggering emergency supplier POs with 10-day lead times and minimum-order quantity surcharges, Demandra generates automated inter-store transfer manifests, routing excess units across municipal store clusters in hours.
